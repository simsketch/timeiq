TimeIQ turns the schedules you get into events on your calendar, and gives people one link to book time with you.

FORWARD ANY SCHEDULE
Forward a soccer schedule PDF, a band email or a team-app screenshot to your TimeIQ address, or share a schedule's text straight from any app. Every game, rehearsal and practice lands on your calendar, and you can undo an import with one tap.

ONE BOOKING LINK FOR ALL YOUR CALENDARS
Connect Google, Outlook and any calendar link. TimeIQ checks all of them before anyone books, so double bookings stop. Set your hours, choose your event types and share your link from the share sheet.

ASK TIMEIQ
Tell the assistant what you need in plain words: "when am I free Thursday?", "find a time with Sam next week" or "cancel tomorrow's 3 pm booking". It reads your calendars and does the work.

SHARE YOUR OPEN TIMES
Pick a few days and the time zones you need, and TimeIQ writes out when you're free, ready to paste into an email or message.
